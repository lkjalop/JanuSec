# Strategic Path Decision: Acquisition vs Fundraising vs Career
## How to Ask David Linthicum for Strategic Guidance

**Critical Context:** You're at a decision point with 3 viable paths. David is the PERFECT person to help navigate this because he's seen hundreds of exits, fundraises, and career pivots.

---

## Part 1: How to Ask David (The Right Way)

### ❌ WRONG Approach (Desperate/Unfocused)

```
"Hi David, I don't know what to do with JanuSec. Should I sell it? Should I raise money?
Should I just get a job? I'm confused and need help deciding."
```

**Why this fails:**
- Too open-ended (David has to do all the thinking)
- Signals indecision (not founder material)
- No clear ask (what does success look like?)

### ✅ RIGHT Approach (Strategic/Focused)

```
Subject: Strategic Advice Request: Acquisition vs Build (15-min call?)

David,

Quick context: I built JanuSec solo in 6 months while interning at an XDR company.
The platform is 78% production-ready and validated by your feedback.

I'm now at a strategic decision point and would value 15 minutes of your time to
gut-check my thinking. You've seen hundreds of exits and fundraises—your pattern
recognition would be invaluable.

**THE QUESTION:**
Given what you've seen in the deck, which path makes most sense for JanuSec?

**PATH A: Acquisition (12-18 months to exit)**
- Sell to existing security vendor (CrowdStrike, Splunk, Wiz, etc.)
- Likely outcome: $2M-$10M exit (acqui-hire + IP)
- Your role: Intro to corp dev at 2-3 strategic buyers

**PATH B: Fundraising (3-5 years to Series A)**
- Raise $500k-$1M seed, build team, scale to $1M ARR
- Likely outcome: $5M-$10M Series A, 5-10 year build
- Your role: Intro to 2-3 enterprise-focused seed VCs

**PATH C: Career Leverage (immediate)**
- Use JanuSec as proof of capability, join top company as founding/staff engineer
- Likely outcome: $300k-$500k total comp + equity at FAANG/unicorn
- Your role: Intro to hiring managers at companies you know

**MY HYPOTHESIS:**
Path A (acquisition) makes most sense because:
1. Solo founder risk is high (burnout, no co-founder)
2. I'm 6-12 months ahead of competitors on explainable AI triage
3. XDR companies (my current employer's space) desperately need this capability
4. $2M-$10M exit lets me de-risk, then do Path B next time with experience

**WHAT I'M ASKING:**
1. Does my hypothesis match what you'd advise given the market?
2. If Path A, who are the 2-3 strategic buyers you'd start with?
3. If not Path A, what am I missing?

15-minute call this week or next? Happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
Kevin

P.S. - The "intern project" origin story is intentional positioning: it shows I can
execute at startup speed inside a slow-moving enterprise. Most acquirers love that narrative.
```

**Why this works:**
- ✅ Concise (200 words, skimmable)
- ✅ Shows strategic thinking (you've done the work)
- ✅ Multiple choice (easy for David to opine)
- ✅ Clear ask (15-min call, specific intros)
- ✅ Respects his time (not "solve my life")

### PATH B: Fundraising (Build a Venture-Backed Company)

**Timeline:** 3-5 years to Series A, 7-10 years to exit

**Fundraising Stages:**

**Stage 1: Friends & Family ($50k-$250k)**
```
What you're buying: 6-12 months runway to prove product-market fit
Milestones:
- 3-5 enterprise pilots (free POCs)
- 1-2 paying customers ($50k-$100k ARR each)
- Proof of 60-80% alert reduction (case studies)
Equity given away: 5-10%
Who invests: Rich uncles, former bosses, angels in your network
```

**Stage 2: Pre-Seed ($250k-$500k)**
```
What you're buying: 12-18 months runway to hit $500k ARR
Milestones:
- Hire 1-2 engineers (can't scale solo forever)
- 5-10 paying customers ($50k-$200k ARR each)
- Repeatable sales motion (prove it's not just you)
Equity given away: 10-15%
Who invests: Seed VCs (Hustle Fund, Uncork, Village Global), YC, Techstars
```

**Stage 3: Seed ($500k-$2M)**
```
What you're buying: 18-24 months runway to hit $1M-$2M ARR
Milestones:
- Team of 5-10 people (engineering, sales, marketing)
- 20-50 customers ($50k-$200k ARR each)
- $1M-$2M ARR
- Proof of 5x ROI for customers (case studies)
Equity given away: 15-25%
Who invests: Seed VCs (Accel, Lightspeed, Greylock seed programs)
```

**Stage 4: Series A ($5M-$15M)**
```
What you're buying: 24-36 months runway to hit $10M ARR
Milestones:
- Team of 30-50 people
- 100-200 customers
- $10M ARR
- Clear path to $50M-$100M ARR (unicorn trajectory)
Equity given away: 20-30%
Who invests: Growth VCs (Sequoia, a16z, Insight Partners)
```

**Total Equity Dilution:** 50-70% after Series A (you own 30-50%)

**Pros of Path B:**
- ✅ **Massive upside** (if unicorn: $50M-$500M+ founder outcome)
- ✅ **Control** (you decide product roadmap, hiring, strategy)
- ✅ **Learning** (become CEO, learn fundraising, team building, sales)
- ✅ **Optionality** (can sell at any stage: $10M seed exit, $100M Series B exit, $1B+ IPO)

**Cons of Path B:**
- ❌ **High failure rate** (90% of startups fail)
- ❌ **7-10 year grind** (fundraising treadmill, constant pressure)
- ❌ **Need co-founder** (solo founder success rate: <5%)
- ❌ **Dilution** (you own 30-50% after Series A, VCs own the rest)
- ❌ **No salary** (pay yourself $0-$80k for first 2-3 years while friends make $300k at Google)

**Best For:**
- Ambitious founders who want to build $1B+ company
- Technical founders with business co-founder (sales/marketing expertise)
- High-risk tolerance (willing to bet 7-10 years on 10% success odds)
