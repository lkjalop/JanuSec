## 💼 Professional Positioning Strategy

### **Option 1: The Honest Modern Developer**

**When to use:** If interviewer seems modern/progressive, or if role emphasizes architecture over implementation.

---

### **Option 2: The Spec-Driven Engineer**

**Frame it as:**
> "I practice **spec-driven engineering**: I design the architecture, write detailed specifications, and then use the most efficient tools - including AI assistants - to implement. My focus is on **system design**, **pattern selection**, and **validation**, not on typing every line manually. For example, in JanuSec, I designed the 4-tier agent system, specified the circuit breaker logic, and then validated the implementation for correctness and security."

**When to use:** More conservative interviewers, or when you want to emphasize design over coding.

---

### **Option 3: The Context Engineer**

**Frame it as:**
> "My expertise is in **context engineering** - designing the right abstractions, selecting appropriate patterns, and orchestrating components effectively. I use AI tools as collaborators: I provide the architectural vision and constraints, the AI handles boilerplate, and I validate outputs. This approach lets me focus on high-value decisions like 'Should we use GraphRAG or vector-only?' rather than 'How do I write this for-loop?'"

**When to use:** Gen AI roles that value prompt engineering and AI orchestration.
